## 📂 Structure

Writeups are organized by **difficulty**, then by **track** (Red Team / Blue Team):

THM-Writeups/
├── easy/
│ ├── redteam/
│ └── blueteam/
├── medium/
│ ├── redteam/
│ └── blueteam/
└── hard/
├── redteam/
└── blueteam/



Each room gets its own folder under the matching difficulty/track, containing a `writeup.md`.
